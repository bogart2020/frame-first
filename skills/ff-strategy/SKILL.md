## Purpose

The loop that makes the other skills improve: log what happened, read it back, and decide what to do next.

## Performance logging

Input by **screenshot** of platform Insights (manual paste as fallback). Track:
- **Shares / sends** — primary. Distribution through a relationship is the highest-value signal.
- **Watch-through %** — primary.
- Saves, follows-from-post, views. **Not likes.**

Write to `data/performance.md` with the post, its hook shape, and one line on what it suggests. Read this back in `ff-ideas` and `ff-critique`.

## Strategy this skill enforces

Concert footage is **audience acquisition, not the revenue engine** — it is muted on TikTok, demoted on Instagram, and revenue-shared on YouTube. Revenue comes from: affiliate gear, LUTs/presets, client event videography, and brand work. Platform ad revenue is a bonus, never the plan.
