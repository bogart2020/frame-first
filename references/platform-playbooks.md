# Platform playbooks

## To write here, per platform (IG, TikTok, YouTube, Facebook)
- Native consumption behavior and what the platform actually rewards
- Originality rules — Meta deprioritizes duplicative/minimally-modified content; adding captions or borders to someone else's video is not transformative
- Monetization status for a **Philippines-based** creator (see ff-strategy for the verified table)
- **Concert footage + copyrighted music** — the critical section:
  - TikTok: audio muted, near-certain. LIVE risks instant cut
  - Instagram: auto-detection demotes reach to followers, or mutes
  - YouTube: Content ID **claim** — video stays up and monetized, revenue to the rights holder. Not a strike
  - Facebook: likely claim + shared revenue, policy not publicly detailed
  - Fair use does not cover concert footage; courts decide case by case
- The workaround: library audio on IG/TikTok, real audio reserved for YouTube

## Myths to actively contradict
No universal posting cadence. No universal optimal video length. More hashtags does not mean more reach. Follower count is decoupled from distribution. Viral is not the same as good.
