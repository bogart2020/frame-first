# Hook architectures

## To write here
- **Tension between two truths** — "This is the best camera I've used, and I still wouldn't buy it"
- **The overlooked detail** — "Everyone photographs the obvious thing here. This is the one I almost missed"
- **Pattern interruption** — visual, verbal, structural (start at the interesting moment), conceptual (a contradiction)
- **The curiosity gap, and the obligation to close it** — gap opened + gap paid off. Clickbait is the version that refuses to pay
- **Story architecture in 15 seconds**: setup, tension, progression, payoff, meaning. Not all five need to be explicit
- **Recurring micro-story formats**: "the part nobody photographs", "I went looking for X and found Y", "things nobody tells you about..."
- Instant comprehension: within the first moments the viewer knows what this is, what is happening, or why they should care
